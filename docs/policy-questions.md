# Production policy questions and blockers

## Proposed declarations currently shown in the prototype

The supplied document provides proposed wording for prototype use. The application labels every declaration **Proposed wording — pending final policy review** and records version `esut-seller-declarations-proposed-v1` with status `proposed-wording-pending-final-policy-review`. Source reference supplied by the user: <https://rp0sd4q07g.ufs.sh/f/8t6otpGzNZFgO3ZxDvutOeGhjWu0vFXf72CYQJL4c3gzBiKl>. This URL is recorded as a proposed source reference, not as evidence of final legal or policy approval.

**Declaration 1 — Accuracy and authenticity** applies to every applicant:

> I confirm that the information and documents I have provided are genuine and accurate to the best of my knowledge.

**Declaration 2 — Verification authorization** applies to every applicant:

> I authorize ESUT Marketplace to verify the information and documents I have submitted. I understand that approval of this application does not automatically activate a store or grant seller permissions.

**Declaration 3 — Business ownership confirmation** applies only to Registered Business Owner:

> I confirm that I am the owner of the registered business identified in this application.

**Declaration 4 — Representative authorization confirmation** applies only to Authorized Representative of Registered Business:

> I confirm that I am authorized to submit this application on behalf of the business identified in it.

The prototype does not display Declaration 3 for students, unregistered individual vendors, or authorized representatives. It does not display Declaration 4 for students, registered business owners, or unregistered individual vendors. Hidden conditional acknowledgements are cleared when the applicant path changes and are not submitted.

## Privacy and legal blockers

The privacy notice remains separate from the declarations and is displayed as **Privacy information pending final policy review**. The Privacy Policy URL is intentionally unconfigured. No separate privacy-consent checkbox is added because the appropriate lawful basis and whether consent is required remain unresolved.

Before production integration, the following decisions remain open:

1. Confirm the identity and contact details of the data controller.
2. Finalize the purposes of collecting each category of applicant information.
3. Determine and document the appropriate lawful basis for each processing purpose.
4. Confirm whether consent is required for any processing activity.
5. Finalize the privacy notice and actual Privacy Policy URL.
6. Define retention for applications, supporting evidence, rejected applications, and review history.
7. Identify authorized reviewers and establish access restrictions.
8. Document third-party processors or recipients handling applicant information.
9. Document applicant rights and the process for exercising them.
10. Confirm the complaint channel and applicable data-protection requirements.
11. Confirm final declaration wording and effective date following policy/legal review.
12. Define the current academic-session identifier and how registration documents are checked.
13. Confirm the authenticated account source and allowed ESUT-community connection evidence.
14. Confirm authoritative status values, closure reasons, and correction workflow.
